## Let There Be Light v0.1.0 Beta

First public beta of LTBL, the all-in-one lighting overhaul for Skyrim SE/AE built for Skyrim MAX.

### Included
- **Eye Adaptation:** glare settles in 1 to 10 seconds by brightness; darkness adapts over 20 to 90 seconds. Measured at your character, identical in first and third person.
- **Torch Bearers:** NPCs and followers draw torches by the real light where they stand. One per group, never inside wooden buildings, no on/off flicker.
- **Candlelight Aura and Tomes:** a wide, soft light around you that grows brighter with each tome found in dungeon loot, full at 10. Tomes are extra loot, never replacing anything, and stop appearing once complete.
- **Community Shaders base:** unlimited lights, inverse-square falloff, screen-space GI, skylighting, volumetrics, PBR, HDR and upscaling, all on by default.

### Install
1. Install `LetThereBeLight-v0.1.0-beta.7z` with Mod Organizer 2.
2. Disable any existing Community Shaders and any ENB.
3. Requires SKSE and Address Library for SKSE Plugins.
4. Press END in game to open the menu.

### Known limitations
- Beta: in-game calibration is ongoing. Eye adaptation timing and brightness values may change.
- Candlelight tome progression is tracked per save from the moment LTBL is installed.

Built on Skyrim Community Shaders (GPL-3.0).
