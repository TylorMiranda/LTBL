## Let There Be Light v0.1.2 Beta

### Torch Bearers
- **Torches light and go out one by one.** Each NPC waits a random time before lighting (up to 12 seconds) and before putting a torch out (5 to 90 seconds), so a town never goes dark all at once.
- **Ready for torch animations.** Once LTBL's torch animations are installed, NPCs reach back, draw the torch and light it, and walk to the nearest water (river, lake, pond, creek, puddle in rain, trough, well or barrel) to douse it, or snuff it on the ground if no water is close. Until then torches change without animation, so nothing wrong plays.
- New settings under Torch Bearers: random wait times, water search distance, and animation timing.

### Install
Install `LetThereBeLight-v0.1.2-beta.7z` with Mod Organizer 2, replacing the previous version.
