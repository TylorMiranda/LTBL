## Let There Be Light v0.1.3-beta

### Torch Bearers: real torch animations
- Four new clips, built from CMU motion capture retargeted to the Skyrim skeleton and keyed by hand:
  - **Light**: reach over the left shoulder, draw the torch, strike flint at the head until it catches, raise to carry.
  - **Snuff on ground**: squat, grind the head into the dirt, stow it over the shoulder.
  - **Douse at shore or puddle**: kneel at the water's edge, plunge and swish the head, flick it dry, stow.
  - **Douse in trough, barrel or well**: lean over, dip and stir, flick, stow.
- NPCs turn to face the water before dousing.
- The torch in hand during a clip is a prop with its own flame and light, so the engine's torch-arm pose can't fight the animation. The real torch is handed over on the exact frame the clip reaches the vanilla carry pose.
- Requires **Open Animation Replacer**. Without it, torches still change, just without animation.

### Notes
- Clips only play when LTBL marks the NPC (Variable10), so the vanilla pick-up idle is untouched otherwise.
- First in-game test build of the animations. Report anything that clips or pops.
