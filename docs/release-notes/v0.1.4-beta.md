## Let There Be Light v0.1.4-beta

### Eye adaptation removed
- LTBL no longer touches eye adaptation. Skyrim's own behavior is back.

### Torch Bearers
- The torch in hand during a clip is built from the torch item itself: its model (so mesh and texture replacers apply), its flame, its light (made by the engine's own torch-light routine, so color, radius and flicker match every other torch), and its burning sound.
- Flint: a flint stone comes out of the belt pouch, is struck four times across the torch head, and goes back.
- Sparks fly at each strike (vanilla metal-spark effect), with a strike sound.
- Vanilla sounds when the torch catches and when it goes out.
- The lighting clip is re-keyed so the flint brushes the torch head on every downstroke.
- If an NPC can't play the animation, or Open Animation Replacer is missing, they don't use a torch at all.
