## Let There Be Light v0.2.0-beta

### New: World Light
- **Neutral, bold color.** Weather and imagespace warm/cold tints removed; colors stronger (adjustable).
- **Calm water glare.** Sun glints and sparkle on water toned down; waterfalls, foam, spray and mist no longer blinding.
- **Smoke and haze obey light.** Smoke, steam, dust and fog were drawn unlit, so they glowed at night. They now darken with day, night and interior light.
- **Living light.** Nirnroots (blue, and red for crimson), glowing mushrooms, Blackreach crystals and torchbugs cast real light.
- All runtime: no plugin, no edited records. Works with any mesh or texture replacers.
